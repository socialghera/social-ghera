ABOUT SOCIAL GHERA

Built for Maharashtra, not just India.

Social Ghera is an independent, MSME-registered digital marketing agency based in Pune — started with one clear idea: medium businesses and service providers deserve full-circle marketing, without hiring a five-person in-house team to get it.

THE NAME

Why "घेरा"?

Ghera is Marathi for encirclement — a complete loop with no gaps in it. That's the idea behind the agency: strategy, content, ads and systems shouldn't sit with separate vendors who never talk to each other. They should move as one circle around the brand, each part reinforcing the next.

In practice, that means:

01

One strategy behind every post, ad and message — not four different plans.

02

One team accountable for results, instead of five vendors pointing at each other.

03

One consistent voice across Instagram, WhatsApp, ads and your website.
