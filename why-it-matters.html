THE SHIFT

Behaviour has moved online even for offline businesses.

People research before they visit

A patient checking a doctor's specialisation, a family comparing hotels, or a parent shortlisting a coaching institute all of it usually starts with a phone screen, not a phone call.

WhatsApp is the new front desk

For most service businesses, WhatsApp has quietly become the first real conversation and a slow or missed reply there costs the same as an unanswered phone call.

Reviews carry more weight than referrals used to

A handful of recent Google reviews can now do the work a decade of word-of-mouth used to do for better or worse.

Being findable matters as much as being good

A well-run hospital that's hard to find online loses ground to an average one that shows up first in a local search.

QUESTIONS WE GET

Common questions from businesses like yours.

We already have someone posting on Instagram why do we need an agency?

Posting and a strategy aren't the same thing. An in-house team member handling it alongside their main job usually keeps the account alive, but rarely has the time to plan campaigns, track what's converting, or run paid ads. Social Ghera runs it as the main job, not a side task.

Is digital marketing worth it if we mostly get business through word of mouth?

Word of mouth still starts the conversation but the person hearing about you almost always checks your Instagram or Google listing before acting on it. A weak online presence can quietly undo a strong referral.

Our staff aren't comfortable being on camera can this still work?

Yes. A lot of what works well for hospitals, institutes and service businesses is facility shots, informational content, patient or client experience stories told without staff on camera, and graphics-led posts. On-camera content is an option, never a requirement.

How is this different from a freelancer or a big agency based out of Mumbai?

A freelancer usually can't cover strategy, content, ads and systems together. A large national agency covers all of it, but runs the same playbook across cities and rarely understands Tier-2/Tier-3 Maharashtra specifically. Social Ghera is built to sit in between full-circle, but local.

How soon do we see results?

Consistent content and a cleaned-up online presence show up within the first month. Paid ads and enquiry growth typically need 6-8 weeks to properly optimise. We share a clear timeline before starting, so there are no surprises either way.
